About X-COIN AIRDROP NETWORK

X-COIN AIRDROP NETWORK is a revolutionary decentralized mining platform that leverages blockchain technology to distribute X-COIN tokens through a sustainable proof-of-work mining algorithm.

How It Works

Users activate their mining accounts with a one-time $10 USDT fee, which serves as both an anti-bot measure and a network security deposit. Once activated, users can begin mining X-COIN at a consistent rate of 10 X-COIN per hour.

The platform features an innovative swap mechanism that allows users to convert their mined X-COIN to USDT at a fixed exchange rate of 50 X-COIN = $40 USDT. This provides a seamless bridge between mining rewards and real-world value.
